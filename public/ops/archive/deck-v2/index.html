Why trust Mamoru

  • You keep control Your wallet is a smart account, owned by a passkey on your device. Mamoru never holds the key that can move your money out.
  • A key with limits Mamoru works through a session key. It can act only inside a written policy and you can revoke it anytime.
  • Built on Uniswap Where the capital works. Mamoru swaps and provides liquidity on Uniswap, nothing else.
  • Half in stablecoins by rule Three positions. Mamoru keeps half of the capital in stablecoins, and the yield becomes stablecoins.
  • No lockup Withdraw stablecoins anytime. Mamoru rebalances the rest.
  • No token Nothing to buy, hold or farm.
