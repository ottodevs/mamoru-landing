How it works

  1. 01 Your wallet

    Connect

    Connect your wallet in one click or log in with your email. Mamoru sets up a dedicated sub-account for your agent.

  2. 02 Mamoru Delegated Account

    Fund

    Deposit the ETH you want to put to work. The agent operates programmatically with that capital, leaving your main wallet untouched.

  3. 03 Funds Allocation

    Allocate

    Mamoru splits the capital into three sleeves on Uniswap: stablecoins, bluechips, and a small mercenary sleeve.

  4. 04 Stablecoin pool

    Harvest

    Yield is harvested and reinvested. It stays at work until you transfer.

FAQs

Who holds the money?

You do. It sits in a smart account under your control. Mamoru does not take custody.

Where does a position live?

On Uniswap. Mamoru quotes, opens, and adjusts positions there transparently.

When do funds leave?

When you transfer. Part or all of the funds leave the allocations and go to your account. Until then, yield is reinvested.

Can I withdraw my funds at any time?

Yes. There is no lockup. Transfer part or all of it to your account whenever you want.

Is there a token?

No.

What happens to the email I used to log in?

It stays strictly with us for account access. We never rent or sell lists, and we hate spam as much as you do.
